# Left Eye Field of View Analysis

## 1. Left Eye Field of View Angles

The four bounding angles defining the viewing frustum for the left eye of a Valve Index virtual reality headset relative to the optical axis are:

* **Outer Angle (Left / Temporal):** $53.2^\circ$
* **Inner Angle (Right / Nasal):** $45.6^\circ$
* **Top Angle (Up):** $50.0^\circ$
* **Bottom Angle (Down):** $50.0^\circ$

**Source:** Valve OpenVR Software Development Kit geometry parameters for the Valve Index headset.

---

## 2. Impact of Using a Symmetrical Field of View

Using a symmetrical field of view of equal surface area would waste rendering power on visual areas physically blocked by the user's nose while reducing outer peripheral vision.
